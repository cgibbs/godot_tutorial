I don't really want to spend any energy on combat, and I like the exploration aspects. I think it could be a game like EMUUROM or Promenade where you interact non-combatively with enemies and learn to utilize them without having the default option of killing them.

It would be fun to do enemy bounces and have enemies sometimes play pitched noises so the player plays a little song for longer bounce chains, like that thing in the new Yoshi game (the one Dunkey did a video about).

Turn a one-way platform sideways in front of a horizontal tunnel to make a one-way passage.

Enemy bounce powerup is implemented. Might make certain enemies deactivate on a timer to prevent bouncing in place to get to the right height, otherwise it'll be too easy.

Airdash can use the knockback function, I'm now realizing.

Fans that push the knight while mid-air could make for more interesting platforming.

Bouncy blocks that aren't enemies, and thus don't require the enemy bounce powerup? Could be fun, and would extend the puzzles a bit and introduce bouncing behavior before the powerup is acquired.

Bombs aren't reset by checkpoint. I'm not sure why that matters yet, but I'm sure speedrunners will figure out something silly to do with that.

There's a bomb glitch that happens when you jump up against a single-tile ceiling and lay a bomb, and it just gets your character above the ceiling. That's too fun to delete from the game, it'll just be a neat hidden feature.
